Real Estate / PropTech

Property Valuation RAG System

Challenge

Real estate firms needed accurate Q&A over hundreds of appraisal PDFs. Standard chunkers destroyed table structure and valuation context.

Solution

Built a layout-aware RAG pipeline with custom PDF parser, semantic chunking, ChromaDB vector store, and Gemini 1.5 Pro grounded generation on GCP.

Business Impact

Reduced appraisal review time by 70%, eliminated manual table re-entry errors, and enabled instant comparable sales lookup across hundreds of documents. Turned a multi-hour analyst workflow into a sub-minute AI query.
